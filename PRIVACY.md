# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Page Time Tracker is a browser extension that shows how much time you spend on websites. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What we record

- The **hostname** of each website you visit (for example, `github.com`). Full URLs, page titles and page content are not recorded.
- The **number of seconds** you spend on each hostname per day.
- Your settings: whether tracking is on, your alert threshold and where you placed the widget.

## Where it is stored

All data is stored locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. The extension makes no network requests, has no analytics and has no accounts.

## How long it is kept

Daily history is deleted automatically after 90 days. You can clear today's data at any time from the extension popup. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data.

## Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data. We have no access to it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
